How it works

Furugi is the Japanese word for secondhand clothes. Japan sorts and resells its used clothing carefully, which is why the pieces are so good.

From Japan to your door

  1. We buy selected pieces from suppliers in Japan.
  2. The parcel arrives in Bhutan and we check every piece for stains, holes and smell.
  3. We wash, grade and measure each piece, then photograph it.
  4. We list it here with its item number, size, measurements and honest condition note.
  5. You order, we confirm the piece is still available, and we deliver.

Sizing

Tags show Japanese sizes, which run about one size smaller than most people expect. Trust the measurements, not the letter. Lay a piece you already like flat and compare.

Returns

Write your own policy here. Many small thrift shops offer an exchange within a few days if the measurements on the page were wrong.
